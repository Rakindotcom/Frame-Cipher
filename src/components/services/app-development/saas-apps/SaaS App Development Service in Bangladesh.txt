What Is SaaS App Development?
SaaS app development is the process of building software that customers access as an ongoing online service instead of installing and managing a separate copy.
A SaaS product usually combines the application itself with customer accounts, tenant management, billing, permissions, analytics, integrations, and infrastructure designed for continuous operation.
SaaS vs Traditional Software
Traditional software may be sold or deployed as a one-time product. SaaS is typically delivered as an ongoing service that customers access through the web or connected applications.
SaaS vs a Standard Web Application
A standard web application can serve one company, one workflow, or a limited group of users. A SaaS platform is designed to serve multiple customers with separate accounts, permissions, billing, and customer data.
Multi-Tenant Software
Multi-tenancy allows multiple customer organizations or accounts to use the same platform while keeping their data and access logically separated.
Subscription-Based Access
SaaS products commonly use monthly, annual, usage-based, or other recurring plans to provide access to the software.
Continuous Product Delivery
Because the product is managed centrally, updates, improvements, fixes, and new functionality can be released continuously as the SaaS evolves.

Multi-Tenant SaaS Architecture
Multi-tenancy is one of the core architectural decisions that separates SaaS from ordinary application development.
Tenant Data Isolation
Each customer organization should have logically separated data, users, settings, and business records according to the application's security model.
Shared Database vs Separate Database
Some products can use shared infrastructure with logical tenant separation. Others may benefit from separate schemas or databases based on scale, security, compliance, and operational requirements.
Schema-Based Isolation
Where appropriate, tenant identifiers and database-level rules can be used to keep customer records logically separated within shared infrastructure.
Organization & Workspace Structure
A SaaS product can support companies, teams, departments, branches, workspaces, or other organizational structures based on how customers use the platform.
Tenant-Specific Configuration
Customers may need their own settings, branding, limits, feature access, integrations, or business rules.
Subdomain & Custom Domain Support
Suitable SaaS products can support dedicated subdomains or connected custom domains for individual customers or organizations.
White-Label SaaS
The same multi-tenant foundation can support customer-specific logos, branding, domains, and configurable front-end experiences.
Scaling Tenant Data
The data model should account for growth in tenants, users, records, storage, reports, and activity without making the platform increasingly difficult to maintain.
