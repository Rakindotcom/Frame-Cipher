Why Choose Website Maintenance?
A website is not finished when it goes live. It continues to depend on software updates, security patches, backups, integrations, content changes, and ongoing monitoring.
Without regular maintenance, small problems can become larger ones. An outdated plugin can create compatibility issues. A failed integration can stop leads or orders. An untested backup may not help when recovery is actually needed.
Professional website maintenance keeps those risks visible and manageable.
The right maintenance plan should protect the website, monitor its health, handle routine changes, and provide a clear response when something goes wrong.
Security Stays Current
Regular updates and security checks help reduce exposure to known vulnerabilities and compatibility problems.
Backups Stay Recoverable
Backups are more useful when they are stored separately and periodically tested for restoration.
Problems Are Caught Earlier
Uptime, performance, forms, integrations, and other important website functions can be monitored before customers report an issue.
Routine Changes Stay Simple
Small content changes, product updates, image replacements, and minor fixes should not always require a new development project.
Maintenance Matches the Website
A WordPress blog, WooCommerce store, Shopify site, and custom Next.js application have different technical needs. Your maintenance plan should reflect those differences.
Platform-Specific Website Maintenance
Different platforms have different update cycles, dependencies, security concerns, and maintenance requirements. We tailor ongoing care around the technology your website actually uses.
WordPress Maintenance
WordPress maintenance can include core, plugin, and theme updates, security checks, malware monitoring, backup verification, performance reviews, and compatibility checks.
WooCommerce Maintenance
WooCommerce stores need additional attention around products, checkout, payments, orders, inventory, shipping, and connected plugins or services.
Shopify Maintenance
Shopify maintenance can include app and theme checks, product and content updates, payment-related monitoring, integration checks, and storefront improvements within the platform.
Wix Maintenance
Wix maintenance can include content updates, responsive checks, forms, bookings, connected apps, ecommerce functions, and ongoing troubleshooting within the Wix environment.
Webflow Maintenance
Webflow maintenance can include CMS content updates, page changes, forms, integrations, responsive adjustments, and ongoing site checks.
Magento Maintenance
Magento stores require more specialized maintenance around platform updates, extensions, security, performance, ecommerce functionality, and integrations.
Custom / Next.js Maintenance
Custom applications need active technical maintenance rather than simple CMS updates.
Depending on the application, support can include dependency updates, API monitoring, security improvements, deployment support, performance monitoring, bug fixes, and ongoing feature development.
We do not apply the same checklist to every platform. The maintenance scope is defined around the website's technology, business importance, integrations, and level of risk.

Frequently Asked Questions
How often should a website be maintained?
Maintenance frequency depends on the platform, update cycle, website complexity, traffic, integrations, and business importance. More active or complex websites generally need closer monitoring and more frequent maintenance.
Do you test website updates before applying them?
Where the platform and environment support a staging workflow, we can review and test updates before moving them to production.
Are backups actually tested?
Our maintenance approach includes recovery testing rather than treating the existence of a backup as proof that recovery will work.
Do you maintain websites built by other developers?
Yes. We start with an assessment of the current website, access, security, backups, dependencies, integrations, and known issues before taking over ongoing maintenance.
What happens if my website goes down?
We investigate the incident, identify the cause, and work to restore critical functionality as quickly as the applicable support plan allows. Where an appropriate tested backup exists, it may be used as part of recovery.
What happens if my website gets hacked?
We assess the incident, identify the affected areas, work to remove the issue where possible, restore the website when appropriate, and address the underlying security weaknesses within the agreed scope.
Is content updating included in website maintenance?
Minor text, image, product, or page updates can be included depending on the maintenance plan. Larger content projects or extensive new page creation are usually scoped separately.
Do you maintain ecommerce websites?
Yes. Ecommerce maintenance can include product updates, checkout checks, payment integrations, order flows, inventory-related systems, performance, and connected apps or plugins.
Do you provide website maintenance for WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, and custom websites?
Yes, maintenance can be structured around supported platforms including WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, Magento, and custom applications such as Next.js.
Does website maintenance include SEO?
Technical SEO checks and maintenance can be included where specified, such as broken links, metadata changes, indexation issues, redirects, and technical problems. A full ongoing SEO campaign is a separate service.
Does website maintenance include a redesign?
No. A redesign changes the website's structure, interface, or user experience substantially and is normally treated as a separate project.
Can I cancel my maintenance plan?
The cancellation terms depend on the maintenance agreement and selected plan. These terms should be clearly stated before the service begins.
Can I request additional work outside my maintenance plan?
Yes. Larger features, redesigns, major integrations, extensive content work, migrations, or development projects can be scoped and quoted separately.
