Why Your Business Needs Real UI/UX Design
UI/UX design is more than making a website or application look modern. It affects how easily users understand the product, complete tasks, and move through important journeys.
Easier User Journeys
Clear navigation and structured user flows help people understand what to do next without unnecessary decisions.
Fewer Friction Points
Confusing forms, unclear actions, difficult navigation, and complicated checkout flows can cause users to leave without reporting the problem.
Better Mobile Usability
A desktop layout cannot simply be reduced in size and expected to work equally well on mobile. Content hierarchy, navigation, spacing, and interaction often need to change.
Lower Redesign Risk
Validating structure before development makes major usability issues easier to identify and resolve before they become expensive changes.
Stronger Product Scalability
Reusable components, consistent patterns, and a clear design system make future features easier to design and maintain.
Better Design-to-Development Alignment
A well-structured design gives developers clearer guidance about layouts, interactions, responsive behavior, and interface states.

UI/UX Design vs Web Design: What's the Difference?
UI/UX design and web design can overlap, but they are not always solving the same problem.
UI/UX Design
	Web Design
	Focuses on how users move through a product
	Focuses more on presenting a business or brand online
	Maps user journeys and task flows
	Structures website pages and content
	Common for apps, SaaS, dashboards, and web applications
	Common for business, marketing, and informational websites
	Includes usability and interaction decisions
	Focuses more on visual page presentation
	Often includes prototypes and testing
	Often connects directly to website development
	A business website may need both. A SaaS product, dashboard, ecommerce experience, or mobile app often requires deeper UX planning because users perform repeated or more complex tasks.
We recommend the appropriate design approach based on what your website or product needs.

Frequently Asked Questions
What's the difference between UI design and UX design?
UX design focuses on how a product works, how users move through it, and how easily they complete tasks. UI design focuses on the visual interface, including typography, colors, spacing, components, and screen appearance. We handle both as part of one connected design process.
Do I need UI/UX design if I'm using WordPress or Shopify?
Yes. Platforms provide a technical foundation, but user flow, information architecture, navigation, visual customization, and usability still need to be considered.
Can you redesign an existing website or app?
Yes. We can audit the current experience, identify usability problems, improve important flows, and redesign selected areas or the full product based on project scope.
Do you provide UX audits?
Yes. UX audits can review navigation, user flows, usability, mobile experience, forms, checkout, interaction patterns, accessibility concerns, and available user behavior data.
Do you conduct usability testing with real users?
Yes, when testing is included in the project scope. We can test prototypes with real or representative users to identify friction before development.
Can you design for a mobile app?
Yes. We apply research, flow mapping, wireframing, and UI design principles while adapting the experience to mobile interaction patterns and platform conventions.
Do you design SaaS dashboards and web applications?
Yes. We design SaaS products, dashboards, customer portals, internal tools, web applications, and other digital products with more complex workflows and multiple user roles.
Will developers receive Figma files and design specifications?
Yes. Depending on the project scope, handoff can include organized Figma files, reusable components, prototypes, responsive layouts, interaction states, design-system references, assets, and development notes.
What will I receive at the end of a UI/UX project?
Typical deliverables can include user flows, wireframes, high-fidelity UI screens, interactive prototypes, design systems, organized design files, and development-ready specifications.
How is UI/UX design different from web design?
UI/UX design focuses more deeply on user behavior, task flows, interaction, usability, and product structure. Web design focuses more on page layout, branding, content presentation, and website communication. Many projects benefit from both.
Does UI/UX design include development?
UI/UX design and development are separate services. However, Framecipher can support the transition from approved design into website or application development where required.
Can you work with our existing development team?
Yes. We can provide design files, prototypes, specifications, and handoff documentation for internal developers or external development teams.
Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?
Yes. We provide UI/UX design for businesses and digital products in Bangladesh and international markets, including the US, UK, Australia, Canada, and UAE.
How long does a UI/UX design project take?
The timeline depends on the number of screens, flows, research requirements, testing, complexity, and review cycles. Smaller projects may take around one week, while complex products can take several weeks or longer.
